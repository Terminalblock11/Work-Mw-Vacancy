How to apply

The poster is built from the same details as the post and updates as you type. Share opens your phone's share sheet: pick WhatsApp or Facebook. The matching post text is copied for you, so paste it as the caption if the app does not fill it in.

WhatsApp version uses *asterisks* for bold. Facebook version is plain text with hashtags.
